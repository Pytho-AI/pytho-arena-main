# Strait Sentinel air-base scenario basis

`Strait Sentinel — Raven Air Base Defense` is an unclassified training surrogate.
Raven Air Base is fictional. Its geometry, aircraft mix, unit markings, sensor sites,
response sites, readiness state, and event timing do not reproduce a real installation.

## Public reporting used

- [CENTCOM, 23 June 2025](https://www.centcom.mil/MEDIA/PUBLIC-RELEASES/Article/4223915/us-and-qatari-forces-successfully-defend-against-iranian-ballistic-missile-atta/)
  reported that U.S. and Qatari forces defended Al Udeid Air Base from an Iranian
  ballistic-missile attack. Raven does not recreate that event or model ballistic
  missiles.
- [CENTCOM, 28 May 2026](https://www.centcom.mil/MEDIA/PRESS-RELEASES/Press-Release-View/Article/4502431/statement-from-centcom-on-recent-iranian-aggression/)
  reported five Iranian one-way attack drones near the Strait of Hormuz and a sixth
  launch prevented. Raven uses a larger synthetic wave so track association and sensor
  handoffs are visible.
- [CENTCOM's 6 April 2026 public fact sheet](https://www.centcom.mil/Portals/6/Documents/Publications/260406-FactSheet.pdf)
  lists fixed-wing aircraft, remotely piloted aircraft, and counter-drone systems used
  in the current regional operation. It does not establish Raven's fictional force mix.

## Visual reference basis

The renderer combines general features visible in official public imagery from Al Udeid
and Al Dhafra. It does not copy either base's map.

- [AFCENT Al Udeid flight-line operations](https://www.dvidshub.net/video/737107/al-udeid-air-base-flightline-operations)
  supports the broad concrete ramps, aircraft servicing, and ground-support clutter.
- [AFCENT Al Udeid runway reopening](https://www.afcent.af.mil/News/Article/3597610/al-udeid-inner-runway-reopens-increases-operational-capabilities/)
  supports the runway, parallel taxiway, pavement markings, and shoulders.
- [AFCENT Al Udeid multi-use hangar](https://www.afcent.af.mil/Units/379th-Air-Expeditionary-Wing/News/Display/Article/628152/auabs-first-multi-use-hangar-opens/)
  supports a wide clear-span maintenance hangar and tanker-scale apron.
- [AFCENT Al Udeid modernization](https://www.afcent.af.mil/News/Article/2202804/auab-evolving-to-be-air-base-of-future/)
  supports permanent dormitory, dining, and mission-support buildings alongside older
  expeditionary structures.
- [USACE logistics complex](https://www.tam.usace.army.mil/Media/News-Releases/Article/2457352/tam-awards-contract-for-logistics-complex-in-the-state-of-qatar/)
  supports generic warehouses, a vehicle facility, dormitory blocks, and utilities.
- [AFCENT Al Dhafra flight line](https://www.afcent.af.mil/Units/380th-Air-Expeditionary-Wing/News/Display/Article/1781082/the-flight-line-at-adab/)
  supports fighter, tanker, reconnaissance, maintenance, and support-aircraft visual
  categories.

## Fictional render specification

Raven uses one runway, one parallel taxiway, four generic connectors, a fighter apron,
a mobility apron, six weather canopies, two maintenance hangars, low tan operations and
support buildings, a control tower, a fire station, warehouses, water tanks, service
yards, floodlight masts, and abstract counter-UAS props. Concrete joints, patched
asphalt, faded markings, rubber, dust, heat haze, sparse scrub, dry drainage channels,
and distant low ridges establish the Gulf-desert setting.

Aircraft and vehicles use fictional markings and a deliberately non-current mix. Fuel
and munitions facilities remain abstract, distant scenery. The scene omits real
coordinates, exact layouts, present-day aircraft counts, unit posture, defensive-system
placement, security routes, storage locations, readiness timing, response timing, and
modeled weak points.

Public sources were checked on 10 August 2026.
